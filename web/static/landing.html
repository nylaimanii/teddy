What Teddy does

Always a hint, never the answer. Teddy helps kids get there themselves.

Find my stuff

Keys, shoes, backpack, bunny. He looks, points his arm, and remembers where he last saw them.

Homework buddy

Small guiding questions, one step at a time on the screen. The kid says the answer, not Teddy.

Show me how

Tie shoes, brush teeth, draw a cat, pack a backpack. One step, then "done!", then the next. Earn a badge.

Read this

Hold up a page and he reads it slowly, then sounds out the tricky words.

Who does what inside Teddy

SnowflakeHis brain. How-to guides and homework notes live in Snowflake and are found with Cortex Search; AI_COMPLETE (llama3.3-70b) plans homework hints and writes the parent's weekly note; Cortex Analyst answers parents' questions; AI_PARSE_DOCUMENT reads the reference PDFs.
Tiger DataA time-series log of every session, skill practiced, find and mood check-in, rolled up with continuous aggregates for the parent charts.
SolanaFinishing a how-to mints a one-of-one "skill badge" on devnet, like "Tied My Shoes!", shown as a QR code on the iPad.
VultrHosts the Parent dashboard and its API, with HTTPS.
ElevenLabsTeddy's warm voice, and a slower one for reading aloud.
GeminiBackup eyes, when the on-device vision model isn't sure.

For parents

A simple weekly page: what your kid worked on, skills they learned, how they felt (mood check-ins, not a diagnosis), and "where are the keys?"

Open the Parent page

Private by default

Mood check-ins store only a feeling word and a time, never a face. Photos Teddy keeps to remember where things are are cropped to the object with faces blurred. The Parent page is PIN-protected.

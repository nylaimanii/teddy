Talk, point, wave. That's the whole interface.

Built for people a touchscreen leaves out: little kids, seniors, and people who are blind or have low vision.

🔑

Finds your things

"Where are my glasses?" He looks, points with his arm, and says "on the left, by the laptop". Or he remembers when he last saw them.

📖

Reads out loud

Hold up a letter or a medicine label and he reads it to you.

✏️

Homework buddy

Works through math one step at a time on screen, and the answers are always checked.

📚

Story time

Illustrated stories on the screen, read aloud in a warm voice.

❤️

CPR coach and first aid

Pumps his arms at 110 beats a minute and answers only from Red Cross, AHA and NIH guidance, naming the source.

💛

Checks on you

Notices a fall, asks if you're okay, and tells your family if you don't answer.

His brain and memory run on Snowflake

Everything Teddy sees, hears and does becomes a row his family can ask about in plain English.

Knows first aidAI_PARSE_DOCUMENT

Red Cross, AHA and NIH guides are parsed, chunked and indexed with Cortex Search.

Answers safelyAI_COMPLETE · llama3.3-70b

Answers only from those sources, short enough to say out loud, and names where they came from.

RemembersSIGHTINGS · EVENTS

Where things were seen (with a photo), moods, check-ins and alerts.

Tells familyCortex Analyst

"How was grandma this week?" becomes SQL, then a warm answer on the caregiver dashboard.

Open Teddy's screen on an iPad next to the bear and tap Wake Teddy. If his computer is offline, you'll get a demo instead.
